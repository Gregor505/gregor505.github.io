Privacy Policy

YouTube Playlist Organizer

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Purpose

YouTube Playlist Organizer is a personal Windows application used to organize and manage videos in YouTube playlists.

Google and YouTube Data

With the account owner's authorization, the application accesses YouTube account and playlist information needed to read playlists, manage video membership, and perform approved playlist operations.

Storage

The desktop application stores authorization credentials, playlist records, and operation progress locally on the owner's computer.

Data Use

Google account data is used to provide the application's playlist-management functionality. The application is intended for personal use.

Sharing

The application is not intended to sell Google user data or share that data with advertisers. Requests are sent to Google's YouTube API services as needed to perform authorized operations.

Revoking Access

The account owner may revoke the application's access through Google Account connections .

Google and YouTube Policies

Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service .

Learn more about Google's data practices in the Google Privacy Policy .
